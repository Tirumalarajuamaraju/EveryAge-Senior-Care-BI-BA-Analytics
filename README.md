# EveryAge-Senior-Care-BI-BA-Analytics
## Senior Care – EHR, HR & Operations BI Analytics

## Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analysis approach for a senior healthcare and senior living organization.

The objective is to design a centralized Business Intelligence reporting solution covering three business domains:

1. Electronic Health Records (EHR)
2. Human Resources (HR)
3. Operations

The solution focuses on translating business requirements into structured KPIs, functional requirements, user stories, data specifications and dashboard reporting requirements.

**Note:** This is an independently recreated portfolio case study. All examples, sample datasets and deliverables are fictional or synthetic and do not contain client-confidential information or actual patient/employee data.

## Business Domains

### 1. EHR Analytics
- Falls monitoring
- Wounds monitoring
- Urinary Tract Infections
- Non-Urinary Tract Infections
- Medication Errors
- Hospital Transfers
- Psychotropic Medication Monitoring

### 2. HR Analytics
- Employee Birthdays and Anniversaries
- Applications
- Core Vacancies
- Diversity Monitoring
- Employee Compensation
- Time to Hire
- Employee Turnover

### 3. Operations Analytics
- CCRC Weekly Reporting
- CCRC Monthly Reporting
- Occupancy Reporting
- Census Monitoring
